3. Common Reasons Indian Exam & Passport Forms Get Rejected

  • Selfie Mirror-Flip or Wide-Angle Distortion: Front-camera selfies flip text/features and exaggerate nose proportions. Always use the rear camera from 4–5 feet away.
  • Signature Signed in CAPITAL LETTERS or Blue Ink: SSC, UPSC, and RRB notifications strictly reject signatures written entirely in block/capital letters or faint blue ink.
  • Dark Shadows or Flash Glare on Forehead: Direct phone flash creates shiny hot-spots on the forehead and nose; shoot facing natural daylight instead.
  • Blurry Resized Thumbnails: Compressing a WhatsApp-forwarded photo multiple times causes heavy JPEG blockiness around the eyes, triggering automatic portal rejection.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (Indian Passport & Exam Standards)

Do I need to print a date on my SSC or RRB exam photo in 2026?

Most recent SSC and RRB notifications no longer require the date printed on the photograph itself, provided the photo was captured within the last 3 months and matches your live webcam capture during OTR registration. Always verify the specific exam notification PDF before uploading.

Can I wear spectacles in my Indian Passport or SSC photo?

No. Both Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) guidelines and SSC/UPSC examination rules advise removing eyeglasses completely to avoid lens glare obscuring your eyes.
