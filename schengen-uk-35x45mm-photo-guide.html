UK & Schengen Visa Photo (35×45 mm) Complete Biometric Rules, Background Shades & 300 DPI Sheet Guide

The 35×45 mm portrait format is the global benchmark established by the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO). It is mandatory for all 29 Schengen Area countries (including France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, and Switzerland), UK Passports and Visas (UKVI), as well as Australian, New Zealand, and Singaporean travel documents. Because European biometric scanners enforce tight crown-to-chin tolerances and specific light-grey backdrop rules, understanding these exact measurements prevents costly VFS Global or TLScontact resubmissions.

1. Official 35×45 mm Specifications: Schengen vs. UK vs. Australia

Although all three jurisdictions share the same 35 mm × 45 mm outer frame, their crown-to-chin zoom and backdrop contrast requirements differ slightly:

3. Five Mistakes That Cause Visa Photo Rejection at European Consulates

  • Over-Brightened Pure White Background: Bright white walls can wash out lighter hairlines or ears; European biometric readers explicitly favor a subtle 10%–15% neutral light grey tone.
  • Hair Obscuring Eyebrows or Side of Face: Swept bangs covering the eyebrows or loose hair hiding the ears will fail facial landmark detection.
  • Eyeglass Reflections or Heavy Rims: HM Passport Office advises removing all glasses; tinted lenses or frame edges crossing the eye line result in automatic rejection.
  • Low-Resolution Inkjet Banding: Printing below 300 DPI or using standard office copy paper produces visible ink dots that fail optical scanning.
  • Head Tilted or Smiling: Even a slight smile raises the cheeks and distorts inter-pupillary biometric ratios.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (UK & Schengen 35×45 mm)

Do I need two identical photos for a Schengen Visa appointment at VFS Global?

Yes, most Schengen consulates require two identical recent 35×45 mm physical photos—one glued to the application form and one loose for biometric scanning. Printing a 4×6 sheet gives you 8 identical copies so you always have spares.

Can I use the same 35×45 mm photo for UK Passport and Schengen Visa?

If your head height is calibrated to 33 mm (which falls inside both the 29–34 mm UK window and the 32–36 mm Schengen window) on a plain light grey background, the physical 35×45 mm print satisfies both standards—provided the photo was taken within the last 30 days for UK rules.
