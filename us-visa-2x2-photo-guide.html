US Passport & Visa Photo (2×2 Inch) Complete Specifications, Framing & 300 DPI Printing Guide

Whether you are submitting a U.S. Passport renewal (Form DS-82), a first-time passport application (Form DS-11), a non-immigrant U.S. Visa application (Form DS-160), a USCIS Green Card petition (Form I-485), or an entry for the Diversity Visa (DV) Lottery, your photograph must pass automated facial-geometry verification. Even a 2-millimeter deviation in head height or a faint shadow behind the ear can trigger an automatic portal rejection or delay your appointment.

1. Official 2026 U.S. Passport & DS-160 Visa Photo Dimensions

The U.S. Department of State enforces identical physical and biometric geometry rules across both physical print submissions and digital online uploads:

3. Top 6 Reasons U.S. Passport & DS-160 Photos Get Rejected

  • Wearing Prescription or Reading Glasses: Even anti-glare glasses cause micro-reflections over the pupils and are banned under U.S. State Department rules.
  • Digital Retouching or Beauty Filters: Smartphone smoothing filters that alter skin texture, moles, or jawline contours violate biometric authenticity standards.
  • Shadows Behind the Head or Under the Chin: Standing too close to a wall casts a dark halo that automated background-detection algorithms flag as a non-uniform backdrop.
  • Incorrect Cropping Ratio (Rectangular Instead of Square): Submitting a 35×45 mm European photo for a U.S. application fails the mandatory 1:1 square aspect ratio check.
  • Uniforms or Camouflage Clothing: Daily civilian attire is required; military, law enforcement, or airline uniforms are prohibited.
  • Recycled Old Photos: Submitting the exact same photo used in a previous passport or visa application older than 6 months results in immediate rejection.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (US 2×2 Photo Standard)

Can babies or toddlers have their eyes closed or hands visible?

For infants under 1 year old, eyes do not have to be fully open, and you may lay the baby on a plain white sheet to photograph from above—provided no supporting hands or toys appear anywhere in the frame.

Is a 2×2 inch photo also valid for Indian OCI, Canadian Visa, or Chinese Visa?

Yes, Indian OCI/Renunciation applications require a 2×2 inch (51×51 mm) white-background photo. However, Canadian Passports require 50×70 mm and Chinese Visas require 33×48 mm—both of which are available as separate presets inside QuickPhotoGrid.
