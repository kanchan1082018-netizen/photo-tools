Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026 | Domain: quickphotogrid.com

At QuickPhotoGrid Studio (accessible from quickphotogrid.com), safeguarding the privacy and security of our global visitors is our foundational commitment. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our web-based passport photo layout, background removal, cropping, and image compression tools.

1. 100% Client-Side Image Processing (Zero Server Uploads)

Unlike traditional cloud-based photo editors, QuickPhotoGrid operates exclusively inside your local web browser using HTML5 Canvas and client-side WebAssembly technologies:

  • No Image Uploads: When you select or drag-and-drop personal portraits, signatures, or identity documents (such as Passport, Visa, Aadhaar, PAN, or Driver's License scans), those files are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Zero Cloud Storage: We do not store, view, log, sell, or retain copies of any photographs or documents you format.
  • Instant Memory Disposal: All image data remains solely within your device's temporary browser memory and is permanently cleared as soon as you refresh or close the browser tab.

2. Standard Log Files and Non-Personal Analytics

QuickPhotoGrid follows standard web hosting procedures by utilizing non-personally identifiable log files and privacy-respecting analytics. Information collected may include anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and device screen dimensions. This aggregate data is used strictly to monitor uptime, prevent abuse, and optimize interface performance across desktop and mobile devices.

3. Google AdSense and DoubleClick DART Cookies

To keep QuickPhotoGrid 100% free for users worldwide without subscriptions or watermarks, we partner with Google AdSense to display non-intrusive advertisements.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to visitors based on their visit to QuickPhotoGrid and other websites across the internet.
  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on QuickPhotoGrid, which are sent directly to users' browsers.
  • QuickPhotoGrid has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or reviewing the Google Advertising & Privacy Policy.

4. Browser Local Storage (Sticky State Preferences)

QuickPhotoGrid may use your browser's built-in localStorage feature to remember your preferred paper layout (such as A4 or 4x6), photo spacing, or theme mode (Light/Dark) between visits. This preference data stays strictly on your own device and can be cleared anytime through your browser settings.

5. GDPR (EEA/UK) and CCPA (California) Privacy Rights

Because QuickPhotoGrid does not require user registration and does not collect or store personal files, we do not maintain personal user databases. However, under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California have the right to control advertising cookies via browser settings or Google's consent management tools.

6. Children's Information

QuickPhotoGrid does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) from children under the age of 13. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise minors while using online document formatting tools.

7. Consent and Policy Updates

By using QuickPhotoGrid, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, please reach out via our Contact Us page or email website1082018.com.
